Between Lines and Feelings
A story of slow, quiet connections...
First Lab Meeting
Narrator: It’s your first programming lab as a freshman CS student. You stare at the screen, confused.

Eli: “Uh… hey. First time coding too?”
You look at him. He seems friendly, calm, kind.
First Study Session
He ends up helping you anyway.
You sit together, going through basic coding tasks.

Eli: “Try typing this… see? Logic is everything. You’ll get it.”
He’s patient. More patient than he should be.
After Class
Weeks pass. He waits for you sometimes after class. You talk. You laugh. You feel something… but also this weird guilt from missing your ex.

Narrator: Eli knows. He knows you're not fully healed. But he stays.
Midterm Week
You’re stressed. Tired. Burnt out.
Eli finds you sitting alone outside the lab.

Eli: “Aya… are you okay? You seem overwhelmed lately.”
His Confession
Eli: “Aya… I like you. More than a classmate. More than a lab partner.”
You freeze. Your heart beats faster. He waits.
Unspoken Lines
Aya: “I’m not ready, Eli. I don’t want to hurt you.”
Eli: “…Thank you for being honest.”
Narrator: You remain classmates. But there’s tension. It becomes a quiet, unresolved ache.

A story that almost became something—but didn’t.
Parallel Paths
Aya: “I’m scared. I hate attention. I don’t want people in class to talk…”
Eli: “Then we’ll take it slow. No pressure. No big announcements. Just us. Quiet and steady.”
Narrator: And he keeps his promise. Years pass. Your names are called. You look at him. He smiles—proud, warm, yours. You grew together—slowly, quietly, beautifully.
